# Privacy Policy for Rebound

**Last updated: May 30, 2026**

Thank you for playing **Rebound** (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains
what information the Game does and does not handle. We have designed Rebound to
respect your privacy: it is a single-player game that runs entirely in your web
browser.

## The short version

Rebound does **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal
information. The Game has no user accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no
advertising. The only data it keeps is your game progress and settings, and
that data is stored **locally on your own device** — it never leaves your
browser.

## Information we do not collect

We do **not** collect or process any of the following:

- Names, email addresses, or other contact details
- Account or login credentials (the Game has no accounts)
- Location data
- Camera, microphone, or photo access
- Device contacts, files, or other personal content
- Advertising identifiers
- Analytics, telemetry, or usage-tracking data

Rebound makes **no network requests**. It does not send any information to us
or to any third party.

## Information stored locally on your device

To make the Game playable, Rebound saves a small amount of gameplay data in
your browser's `localStorage`. This information stays on your device, is
accessible only to the Game in your browser, and is never transmitted anywhere.

The data saved includes:

| Stored item            | Purpose                                              |
|------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| `rebound_settings`     | Your in-game settings (e.g. audio and music levels)  |
| `rebound_audio_muted`  | Whether you have muted the Game's audio              |
| `rebound_best_endless` | Your best score in Endless mode                      |
| `rebound_best_time`    | Your best survival time                              |
| `rebound_progress`     | Your overall game progress                           |
| `rebound_unlocks`      | Which relics/items you have unlocked                 |

This is not personal information — it only describes your progress and
preferences within the Game.

### How to delete this data

You are always in control of this locally stored data:

- Use the **Reset Progress** option in the Game's settings menu, or
- Clear your browser's site data / `localStorage` for the page hosting the
  Game.

Clearing this data will reset your saved progress, settings, and unlocks.

## Cookies and tracking technologies

Rebound does **not** use cookies, web beacons, pixels, fingerprinting, or any
other tracking technologies.

## Third-party services

Rebound does **not** integrate any third-party services such as analytics
providers, advertising networks, social media plugins, or cloud storage. No
third party receives any data through the Game.

If you access the Game through a website or platform operated by someone else
(for example, a game-hosting site or app store), that platform may have its own
privacy practices that are outside our control. Please review the privacy
policy of any platform you use to access the Game.

## Children's privacy

Rebound is suitable for general audiences. Because the Game does not collect
any personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal
information from children. No personal data about children — or anyone else —
is gathered or transmitted.

## Security

Because Rebound stores data only on your own device and transmits nothing, your
gameplay data is as secure as your device and browser. We recommend keeping
your browser and operating system up to date.
